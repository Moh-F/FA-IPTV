# Privacy (FA IPTV)

- No tracking, no analytics, no advertising, no accounts with us.
- Your IPTV logins, favorites, history and settings are stored only on your device (app-private storage, not included in Android backups).
- The app connects only to: the IPTV servers you enter, image/EPG addresses from your playlist, and GitHub (update check).
- The error log stays on the device. Passwords, usernames in stream links, e-mail and IP addresses are removed before writing.
- The optional home-network page is off by default, works only from private IP ranges and needs a one-time token.
- FA IPTV does not provide any channels or media; you are responsible for the content of your own subscription.
